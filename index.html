GLOSSARY
phase — statistical DAX market regime (bull / bear / transition; CRASH = breadth override)
breadth — share of the stock universe above its own 200-day average (0–1; ≤ 0.40 = crash territory)
ret21 — DAX return over the last 21 trading days (%)
target alloc — equity share suggested for the current market phase (%, guidance only)
vix-eq — annualized 21-day DAX volatility (VIX-style; higher = more nervous market)
DAX (source) — index level and day change; the bracketed source is intraday or daily close
MOM / BPP — the two strategy books (momentum / bear-protector); as Top-10 zones: which book's entry gate the candidate fits
zone — which book's entry gate the candidate currently fits
num — number of stocks in the sector (sector table)
RS7d / RS30d — sector's relative strength vs the DAX: sector return minus DAX return, 7d and 30d windows (%)
score — weighted momentum score, 0–100 (sectors: sector average; Top-10: composite technical score of the stock)
dist200 — distance of the price from its 200-day average (%)
rsi — Relative Strength Index (14d), 0–100; >70 overbought, <30 oversold
63d% / rank63 — 63-trading-day return and its rank among the universe
entry / cur / stop — purchase price, current price, stop-loss trigger (€)
stop dist — distance from current price down to the stop-loss (%)
weight — the lot's share of the book's total value (%)
pnl — realized profit/loss of the trade (€, net of commission)
lot — purchase block number within the position (pyramiding = multiple lots)
